About Zibno

I make guides for mobile shooters, with a focus on games that launch in China or in closed beta before they reach the rest of the world.

What I do

When a new shooter appears on mobile, the menus are often only in Chinese and the download steps are unclear. I show how to get the game, then go through the settings screen by screen and explain what every option does in plain English, so you can set up your controls properly on day one.

My channel, Zibno Official, has about 6.9K subscribers and 682 videos. My most-watched guides cover Valorant Mobile, Delta Force Mobile and High Energy Heroes, and one video has passed 90K views.

How guides are made

  • Each written guide is based on one of my own videos and follows what I explain in it, or on official sources, which are listed at the end of the guide.
  • I play on my own phone. I do not publish settings I have not looked at myself.
  • When a game updates, menus and options can change. Each guide shows the date it was last updated and links to its video so you can check.
  • If I am not sure what an option does, I say so instead of guessing.
  • News pages say what is officially confirmed and what is not. I do not post rumours as facts.
  • I remove or rewrite old guides that are no longer accurate or useful.

Corrections

If something in a guide is wrong or out of date, tell me and I will fix it and update the date on the page.

Sponsorships and affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links, such as the booster app I use for the China versions of games. I only link tools I use myself, and I mark affiliate links where they appear. Sponsors do not get to review or change my guides. For brand deals and collaborations, see the contact page.
