About Zibno

I make guides for mobile shooters, with a focus on games that launch in China or in closed beta before they reach the rest of the world.

What I do

When a new shooter appears on mobile, the menus are often only in Chinese and the download steps are unclear. I show how to get the game, then go through the settings screen by screen and explain what every option does in plain English, so you can set up your controls properly on day one.

My channel, Zibno Official, has about 6.9K subscribers and 682 videos. My most-watched guides cover Valorant Mobile, Delta Force Mobile and High Energy Heroes, and one video has passed 90K views.

How the guides on this site are made

  • Each written guide is based on one of my own videos and follows what I explain in it.
  • I play on my own phone. I do not publish settings I have not looked at myself.
  • When a game updates, menus and options can change. Each guide links to its video so you can check the date.
  • If I am not sure what an option does, I say so instead of guessing.
