Top 7 mobile shooting games to play in 2024

By Zibno · 11 June 2024

Seven mobile shooters worth playing in 2024: Delta Force Mobile, Operation Apocalypse, Mission Zero, Arena Breakout, Call of Duty: Mobile, Badlanders and Lost Light, with developer and what each one is.

These are the mobile shooters I recommended in mid-2024, from tactical FPS to extraction looter-shooters. Some were still in beta in China at the time.

1. Delta Force Mobile

Developer: TiMi Studio Group. A tactical first-person shooter that brings the classic Delta Force series to mobile, with both PvE and PvP modes. See my Delta Force Mobile requirements guide.

2. Operation Apocalypse

Developer: NetEase Games. A Valorant- and Hyper Front-style shooter that was still in beta. It runs smoothly and the graphics are excellent. You need a Chinese account to log in.

3. Mission Zero

Developer: NetEase Games. A stealth action game with shooting, where you play agents and spies in a high-stakes game of cat and mouse.

4. Arena Breakout

Developer: Tencent Games. A hardcore tactical FPS with realistic weapon handling, a focus on survival, detailed maps and intense firefights.

5. Call of Duty: Mobile

Developer: TiMi Studio Group (Tencent Games). A major FPS with regular updates, lots of shooting modes and battle royale.

6. Badlanders

Developer: NetEase Games. A looter-shooter where you fight for valuable loot and try to escape the battlefield, mixing shooting with survival.

7. Lost Light

Developer: NetEase Games. A survival shooter set in a post-apocalyptic world, where you scavenge for resources, with realistic combat and tactical play.

First posted on my old blog in June 2024.
