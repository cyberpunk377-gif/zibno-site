Delta Force Mobile beta first look: Unreal Engine 5 graphics and 90 FPS

By Zibno · 9 June 2024

My first look at the Delta Force Mobile beta in June 2024: Unreal Engine 5 graphics, 90 FPS from the start, the gameplay seen in early tester footage and how the community reacted.

The Delta Force Mobile beta went out to selected players, and the game is built on Unreal Engine 5. One of the early testers, uwaazy on X and YouTube, shared full gameplay, which gave us our first proper look.

Graphics and performance

The graphics stand out from the first screen: lifelike textures, dynamic lighting and detailed environments. The game runs at 90 FPS from the start, which makes it smooth and responsive.

Gameplay

The footage shows varied missions: tactical stealth, big firefights and strategic planning. The AI is smart and keeps you thinking. The interface is easy to use, the touch controls are responsive and you can customise the layout.

Community reaction

The reaction was very positive. Players were most impressed by the performance, and many said it feels like a console game in your hand.

Tip Delta Force Mobile has since launched worldwide. For phone specs and syncing with PC, read my requirements and cross-progression guide.
