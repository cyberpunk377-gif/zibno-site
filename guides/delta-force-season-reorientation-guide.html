Delta Force Season 11 "Reorientation": everything new (Rover, Layali Grove 2.0, The Mog)

By Zibno · 29 October 2026

The Delta Force 2nd anniversary season explained: new Support operator Rover and his dog, Layali Grove 2.0, the new Warfare map The Mog, MDR and Thompson, Amphibious LSV, fishing and the mobile changes.

Related video on my channel

Season 11 of Delta Force, called "Reorientation", is the game's second anniversary season. It went live on 8 September 2026 on PC, Android and iOS at the same time. It is a big one: a new operator, a reworked Operations map, a brand-new Warfare map, two new guns, a new vehicle and a lot of changes for mobile players. Here is everything, sorted so you can find what matters to you.

New operator: Rover (Support)

Rover, real name Rowan Walsh, is a Support operator built around a military dog. His kit:

Man's Best Friend (gadget)
A dog whistle calls in a military dog. If an enemy is in range, the dog locks on to the nearest one, tackles them and marks them for your team. With no enemies around, it follows you on alert.
Dart Rifle
Fires darts that leave a lingering mist. In Operations the mist heals your allies' injured limbs, while lowering enemies' maximum HP and damaging them over time. In Warfare it has the same effects on enemies, without the healing.
Irritant
A smoke grenade that makes enemies inside it cough, which gives away their position.
Guard Drive and Prey Drive (Operations)
Two passive options. Both make enemies hit by his gadgets easier for the dog to track. Guard Drive gives longer downed time and removes several limb-injury stacks when you rescue an ally. Prey Drive refills a rescued ally's ammo instead.

Tip The October 15 balance update already changed the dog: in Operations, calling more dogs splits the damage between them (20 damage with one, 10 with two, 7 with three) and makes them take more damage, and each dog now lasts 40 seconds instead of 50. One dog that sticks to its target is usually the better play.

Layali Grove 2.0 (Operations)

Layali Grove has been reworked rather than just patched:

  • Three new areas: Serenity Bay, the North Slope Caves and the Haavk Underground Dock.
  • Bigger map borders, a reworked river network and changed cover.
  • A new flagship task, "Bounty: Wave Breaker": steal an Assault Boat from the Haavk Underground Dock and escort it. You can keep using the boat afterwards.
  • Four Jet Skis on the map, plus two more when the task is not active.
  • New characters, Joe Reed, Zola Zar and Taj Faris, with story moments you can interact with.
  • Redesigned insertion points. The blocked area at the Haavk WSR Lab is gone, and the jamming-zone blockage at the Radar Station is open.
  • Faster train extraction at Transnova Station: the door opens 1 minute after you pull the lever, and extraction is ready 3 minutes later instead of 5.

The Mog: new Warfare map

The Mog is a smoky city map set in Mogadishu. It plays as a push-cart tug-of-war with UH-60 Black Hawk suppressive air support, which fits the Black Hawk Down campaign that Delta Force is known for.

Other map changes

  • Space City: 15 minutes into a match, a random third Switch Point appears, either the Black Chamber or the Buoyancy Lab. Pull any two to open the helicopter extraction.
  • On every map you can now mark Switch Points and extraction points inside jamming zones.

New weapons, ammo and attachments

MDR Assault Rifle
A new rifle in 7.62x51mm.
Thompson SMG
A new submachine gun in .45 ACP.
New ammo
.45 ACP Super Permanent, 4.6x30mm CT (aimed at limbs, low penetration), FMJ ST and AP ST, and a 12 gauge dart round.
Attachments
A new permanent APX Hybrid Sight, plus limited-time Haavk parts for the M700, QJB201 and 93R.

New vehicle: Amphibious LSV

A light vehicle that also works on water, in three versions: Assault, Mortar and Rocket Launcher. The Mortar and Rocket Launcher versions are unlocked with points during the match, and you refill the main ammo at supply stations. It replaces several Assault Vehicles and AAVs on Warfare maps such as Cyclone, Fault, Ascension and Akh Canal.

New modes and events in the season

  • Polaris Patrol: a limited-time Operations mode where you can play as the boss Desmoulins. Full breakdown in the Polaris Patrol guide.
  • 1v1 (Operations): duel a friend with your own operator and loadout. It arrived with the mid-season update, and no gear is used up.
  • Free Battlefield (Warfare): custom Victory Unite rooms. Victory Unite also gets commander candidates, and vehicles now need the commander's approval (approved automatically if they do not answer).
  • Fishing and the EDC Tool Kit: both unlock at Operations level 17. You can fish on Zero Dam, Layali Grove and AZ3 (Easy).

Changes for mobile players

  • A new "Maintain Movement in Backpack/Map Screen" setting, so you keep moving while you check your backpack or the map.
  • You can change how see-through the voiceprint indicator is in the custom controls.
  • The M250 LMG has much more recoil on mobile.
  • Mid-season: more recoil on the QJB201 LMG and less recoil on the M14 marksman rifle, both on mobile.

Gameplay and balance highlights

  • Drag Rescue: you can drag a downed teammate to safety. In Warfare every operator can do it.
  • Snipers in Operations get a big spread penalty when you move while aiming.
  • You can now customise your hip-fire crosshair and share it with a code.
  • Hackclaw's Signal Decoder cooldown is down from 120 to 90 seconds. Toxik's Adreno-Boost range is up from 13 to 20 metres. Vlinder's rescue drones heal 50 HP instead of 20 in Operations.
  • Flashbangs and stun gadgets now do almost no damage (0.01 instead of 1).

Season progress reset

All Season 10 mission progress was cleared, and Season Access to the Ultimate Safe Box and trial cards expired. The Season 11 mission line is shorter than usual: fewer stars are needed in phase two, there are fewer combat missions, and you only need 1 Collector submission for Season Access (5 for the deluxe appearance).

FAQ

When did Delta Force Season 11 start?

Season 11 "Reorientation" went live on 8 September 2026 on PC, Android and iOS.

Who is the new operator in Delta Force Season 11?

Rover (Rowan Walsh), a Support operator who calls a military dog that tackles and marks enemies.

What is new on Layali Grove 2.0?

Three new areas (Serenity Bay, North Slope Caves, Haavk Underground Dock), a bigger map, the Wave Breaker boat task, Jet Skis and faster train extraction.

What are the new guns in Season 11?

The MDR assault rifle in 7.62x51mm and the Thompson SMG in .45 ACP.
