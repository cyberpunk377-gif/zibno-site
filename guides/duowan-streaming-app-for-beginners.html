Duowan: the best app to start streaming from your phone as a beginner

By Zibno · 9 June 2024

Duowan lets you record and stream your phone screen with just a charging cable, for about $10 a month: what it does, why it suits beginners, and StreamChamp as a free option for iPhone.

If you are starting out as a streamer, you do not need an Elgato capture card. Duowan lets you record your screen and stream straight from your phone, and it costs about $10 a month.

What is Duowan?

Duowan is an app that records your phone screen and streams it using only your phone and a charging cable. That makes it ideal if you are just starting and do not have a budget for streaming gear, whether you game, vlog or make tutorials.

Key features

Affordable
About $10 a month, without cutting quality.
Easy setup
Just your phone and a charging cable. No extra hardware.
HD recording
Records your screen in high definition.
Streaming
Stream from your phone to popular platforms in real time.

StreamChamp for iPhone

On iPhone, StreamChamp is a good companion to Duowan. It adds a watermark, but it streams without any extra equipment, so it is a simple, cheap way to start.

Why it suits beginners

  • Cheap: much less than a capture card while you test the waters.
  • Simple: no complicated setup, so you can focus on content.
  • Flexible: works for tutorials, gaming and live events.
