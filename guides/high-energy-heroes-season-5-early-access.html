High Energy Heroes Season 5 early access: how to apply for the new version test

By Zibno · 8 June 2024

How to apply for the High Energy Heroes new version experience test: the Chinese keyword to send on the official channel, the questionnaire, and why a QQ account improves your chances.

High Energy Heroes announced a new version on its official channel, and some players can try it early. Here is how to apply and improve your chances of being picked.

How to apply

  1. Go to the game's official channel page and open the link that takes you to the messaging section.
  2. In the text box, paste this Chinese phrase: 新版本体验 ("new version experience"). Do not write anything in English, or you will not get a reply. Hit send.
  3. After a moment you get a reply saying your code was received, with a link to the questionnaire.
  4. Open the link and fill in the form carefully. Double-check your details.
  5. Wait. If you are selected, you are told in the same chat or by email.

Tip From past tests, players who apply with a QQ account seem to get picked more often than WeChat players. If you have QQ, use it. Here is how to create a QQ account.

What is in the new version

There was no official release date yet, but leaks on Bilibili showed a new map that looks like Kings Canyon.
