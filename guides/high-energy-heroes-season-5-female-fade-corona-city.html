High Energy Heroes Season 5 revealed: Female Fade abilities and the new Corona City map

By Zibno · 21 June 2024

The Season 5 reveal for High Energy Heroes: the new legend Female Fade and her passive, tactical and ultimate abilities, plus the real new map, Corona City, with its new gravity cannons.

High Energy Heroes is getting a new legend that is bound to start some debates: a female version of Fade. Whether you are a Fade main or just want something fresh, here is what she does and how she compares with the original Fade and Alter. There was no official gameplay footage at the time of the reveal.

Female Fade abilities

Passive: Acceleration Effect
Triggers at the end of her slide and lets her move like a ghost, lowering enemy hit rates. Great for dodging bullets and confusing enemies, much like Fade in Apex Legends Mobile.
Tactical: Rapid Displacement
Push and change direction twice. It works for attacking and defending: dodge enemies, make space for teammates, push in or fall back.
Ultimate: Space Backtracking
Teleport back to a previous position and stay invincible while it happens. Afterwards she leaves a one-way portal that teammates can use. Perfect for escaping, ambushing or rescuing allies.

She is a mix of Fade and Alter: speed, agility and team support in one kit, which makes her a legend worth watching.

The real Season 5 map: Corona City

I had shared a video about a rumoured Season 5 map based on leaks, but that turned out to be false. The developers have now revealed the real map. Its name translates as Corona City (or Sunmian City).

Its standout feature is gravity cannons, but not the ones you know: these have new functions that add a fresh layer of strategy. Several other new map elements were teased too, with details still under wraps at the time.
