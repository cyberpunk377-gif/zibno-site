How to create a QQ account to play Chinese games

By Zibno · 8 June 2024

Step by step: create a QQ account in the QQ app with your phone number, so you can log in to Chinese Tencent games like Valorant Mobile China, Delta Force and High Energy Heroes.

Most Chinese Tencent games ask you to log in with QQ or WeChat. Making a QQ account in the QQ app is quick. Here is how.

Steps

  1. Download the QQ app by Tencent from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the app and tap Sign Up (or Create an Account) on the login screen.
  3. Choose your country code from the list.
  4. Enter your phone number, check it, and tap Next.
  5. Enter the verification code QQ sends you by SMS.
  6. Create a strong password with letters, numbers and symbols, and confirm it.
  7. Add your nickname, date of birth and gender, then tap Next.
  8. Complete the security check to show you are not a robot.
  9. Read and accept the terms.
  10. Tap Register (or Complete) to finish.
  11. Log in with your phone number and password.
  12. Add a profile picture and the rest of your profile if you want.

Your QQ account is now ready to use in Chinese games. For the full process of getting Valorant Mobile China, including real-name verification and a Chinese Apple ID, see how to download Valorant Mobile China from outside China.
