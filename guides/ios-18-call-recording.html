iOS 18 brings call recording to iPhone: how it works

By Zibno · 11 June 2024

iOS 18 adds call recording to the iPhone Phone app: how to record a call, where recordings and transcripts are saved, the consent notice, iCloud sync and what to know about local laws.

Call recording is one of the most requested features in iPhone history, and iOS 18 finally adds it. You can record phone calls right in the Phone app.

Key features

Built in
It is part of the Phone app, so you do not need a third-party app.
Privacy and consent
Everyone on the call is told that it is being recorded, to help with legal requirements in different countries.
Storage
Recordings are saved securely on the device. You can play, share or delete them.
Transcription
Recorded calls are transcribed automatically, which is handy for meetings, interviews and important calls.
iCloud sync
Recordings can sync through iCloud, so you can open them on your iPad or Mac and they stay backed up.

How to use it

  1. During a call, tap the record button on the call screen.
  2. Find your recordings and transcripts afterwards (they are saved to the Notes app), where you can play, share or delete them.

When it is useful

  • Work: record meetings, interviews and client calls so you do not miss details, and share notes from the transcript.
  • Personal: keep memories, document verbal agreements or remember important information.

Tip Call recording laws differ by country and state. The consent notice helps, but it is still your responsibility to follow the rules where you live.
