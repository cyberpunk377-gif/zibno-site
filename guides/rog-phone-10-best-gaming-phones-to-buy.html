Is there a ROG Phone 10? The best gaming phones to buy instead

By Zibno · 1 November 2026

Asus has no new ROG Phone for 2026. Here is what happened, which gaming phones to buy instead, including the RedMagic 12 Pro, and the coolers and controllers worth adding.

If you have been waiting for the ROG Phone 10, stop waiting for now. Asus has said it has no new phones planned for 2026, so there is no ROG Phone 10 to buy this year. You may still see ROG Phone 10 spec pages and comparisons online, but treat those as unconfirmed. This guide explains what happened and what to buy instead.

Tip I have not tested these phones or accessories myself. This guide is put together from published reviews and official pages, all listed under Sources at the bottom. Prices change often and differ by country, so check the price where you live.

What happened to the ROG Phone?

In January 2026, reports said Asus had told partners it would not launch new phones this year. Asus then confirmed there would be no new models in 2026, and chairman Jonney Shih said the company would put its resources into AI products instead.

  • Asus has not said the ROG Phone line is finished for good. It is an open-ended pause, and nobody knows if it will return in 2027.
  • Asus says warranties, repairs and software support for existing phones will continue, so a ROG Phone you already own is not suddenly unsupported.
  • I could not find any official news of a comeback as of November 2026.

RedMagic 12 Pro: the one to watch

This is the big one right now. RedMagic lists the global launch for 3 November 2026, and its specs are a step above anything else in this guide:

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip, plus RedMagic's own R5 gaming chip.
  • 8,400mAh battery with 80W wired and 80W wireless charging.
  • 6.85-inch AMOLED screen at 185Hz.
  • Dual-loop liquid cooling.

Some reports put the global price at $899, but that was not official when I wrote this. One report says the open sale starts on 18 November. The Chinese model is called the 12 Pro+, and specs can differ by region, so check the listing for your country. I will update this page once the price is confirmed.

RedMagic 11S Pro: the safe pick today

GSMArena's gaming phone guide, updated in October 2026, makes this its top choice, mainly because almost no other flagship is built for gaming. It went global in May 2026 with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Leading Edition chip, a 7,500mAh battery, 80W wired and wireless charging, a 6.85-inch 144Hz OLED screen and shoulder triggers. Reports put the starting price at around $799 to $849.

One honest warning: that same guide calls the cooling a gimmick and says sustained performance is unimpressive. If you play long ranked sessions, wait for proper lab tests of the 12 Pro before you spend the money.

RedMagic 11 Pro: best value

This is the older model, and that is the appeal. RedMagic listed it at $699, and it has a 7,500mAh battery, a 144Hz OLED, shoulder triggers, a 3.5mm headphone jack and a fan with liquid coolant. It should get cheaper once the 12 Pro arrives. GSMArena's review found the active cooling makes a real difference, but called the cameras weak and the English in the software awkward. It also has no eSIM.

ROG Phone 9 Pro: the last ROG

Launched in November 2024, it has a Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, a 5,800mAh battery, 65W charging and 15W wireless charging. The screen runs at 165Hz, or up to 185Hz in selected games, although Tom's Guide could not find a game that used 185Hz in its testing. Listed prices vary a lot, and it could get harder to find now that no replacement is coming. It only makes sense at a good price, because RedMagic now offers a newer chip and a bigger battery.

Do you need a phone cooler?

If your phone drops frames after 20 minutes of a shooter, a clip-on cooler can help. Heat is usually what slows a phone down in long sessions, and these use a cold plate and a fan to pull heat from the back of the phone.

  • RedMagic Magnetic Cooler 6 Pro: RedMagic claims 30W of cooling and very low temperatures. The -16°C figure is a best-case number, not what you should expect. Magnets only hold on select RedMagic phones, and other phones use clips.
  • Black Shark coolers: the FunCooler 5 and Magnetic Cooler 6 Pro are popular picks in buying guides, but most of those guides are affiliate sites, so check real user reviews first.

Before you buy, check three things: it needs its own power through USB-C, magnets usually hold best with your case off, and a very cold plate can cause condensation in humid weather.

Do you need a controller?

Shooters like Valorant Mobile are built around touch controls, so a controller is more useful for cloud gaming, emulators and story games. Also check that your game allows controllers before you buy one.

Razer Kishi Ultra

FAQ

Is the ROG Phone 10 coming out?

Not in 2026. Asus has confirmed it has no new phones planned this year, and it has not announced anything for 2027.

Has Asus stopped making phones forever?

Asus has not said that. It calls this a pause, and it says support for existing phones will continue, but there is no return date.

Is the RedMagic 12 Pro better than the ROG Phone 9 Pro?

On paper, yes: it has a newer chip, a much bigger battery and faster charging. Real-world tests were not out when I wrote this, so check reviews after launch.

What is the best gaming phone right now?

The RedMagic 11S Pro is the top choice in GSMArena's October 2026 guide. The RedMagic 12 Pro should take over once it is on sale and tested.

Is a phone cooler worth it?

It can be if your phone gets hot and drops frames in long sessions. If you play in short bursts, you probably do not need one.
