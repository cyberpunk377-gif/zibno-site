Settings glossary

Valorant Mobile

Aim assist
Helps you land shots. Useful when you are starting out. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Big map names
Never, only during the buy phase, or always. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Crouch + scope fire
Crouch and aim down sights at the same time. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Smart fire, set per weapon
Crouch fire
You crouch and fire together. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Smart fire, set per weapon
Left-hand view
Flips the layout for left-handed players. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Location name
Shows where you are, like A site, mid or B site. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Map expand method
Open the big map with its own button, or by tapping the minimap. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Quick rope hold
Smoother control on ziplines and ropes. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Radar mode
Local shows only the area around you, global shows the whole map. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Rotation perspective
Decides whether the fire or scope button also turns your view when you tap or hold it. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Scope fire
The gun fires the moment you aim down sights. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Smart fire, set per weapon
Scope mode and crouch: Hold
It stays on while you hold the button and stops when you let go. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Scope mode and crouch
Scope mode and crouch: Mixed
Both work, a quick tap toggles and a hold acts as hold. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Scope mode and crouch
Scope mode and crouch: Tap
Tap once to turn it on, tap again to turn it off. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Scope mode and crouch
Smart fire: Off
No smart fire, you control everything. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Smart fire, set per weapon
Smart weapon system
Puts you back on your previous or strongest weapon as soon as you respawn, so you are never fumbling through weapons at the start of a fight. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Sniper auto-scope
Scopes in for you the moment you fire a sniper. Good for quick snipes. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings
Standard walk
Adds a whole second joystick that only walks. Use that stick whenever you want to move without noise. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Walk settings
View mode
The minimap either rotates with you or stays fixed in one direction. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Vision cone
A triangle on the minimap that shows where you are looking. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Map settings
Walk
Adds a separate walk button. Tap it and you move silently in any direction with the joystick. It switches off by itself as soon as you fire or crouch. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Walk settings
Walk circle
The walk is built into the joystick. You slide the joystick only a little to walk. It saves screen space, but it can feel tricky when you are new. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Walk settings
Weapon inspection
Shows an inspect button. Faster trick: swipe your melee or weapon icon left or right to inspect. Read more: Valorant Mobile basic settings explained › Advanced settings

Delta Force

Basic
Aim assist (on by default), scope behaviour, heavy weapons like shotguns and snipers, perspective toggles and sprint options. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Comms
Marking and quick messages, for both modes. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Controls
Gyroscope, sensitivity and fire button placement. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Downloads
Download map assets over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Gameplay
Pickup rules. On the Battlefield side also vehicle sensitivity and vehicle FOV. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Graphics and sound
The important one. Turn on 144 FPS here if your phone supports it, and set graphics quality. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
HUD layout
A separate custom layout for each mode. Press the green button to save before you leave. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Sensitivity
Separate values for both modes. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section
Vehicles
Vehicle HUD, sensitivity and gyro steering if you like to drive with gyro. Read more: Delta Force Mobile (China) settings in English › Settings, section by section

High Energy Heroes

Auto assist
Switch weapons automatically when you run out of ammo, and keep throwing grenades like arc stars and thermites. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Advanced settings
Automatic fire
Fires by itself as soon as your aim is on an enemy. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
Fire button rotation
The fire button also turns your view. Helps 2-finger players. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Advanced settings
Graphics
Put FPS on the highest option for smooth play. The rest is preference. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › HUD, sensitivity and graphics
Gyroscope
Off, while firing, scope only, or always on. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
HUD
Tap the first preset for a ready 3-finger or 4-finger layout. TDM has its own layout, and you can copy your classic layout across. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › HUD, sensitivity and graphics
Left-hand fire button
Turn it on if you are left-handed. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
One-tap scope and fire
Scopes in and fires together. Very useful for 2-finger players. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
Scope (ADS) mode
Hold, tap, or both. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
Sensitivity
Set it to your own feel. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › HUD, sensitivity and graphics
Single-shot sniper and semi-auto shotgun
Fire on press, or on release. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Basic settings
Third-person view
Set it to 100% for a wider view of the area. Read more: High Energy Heroes settings in English (Apex Mobile 2.0) › Advanced settings
