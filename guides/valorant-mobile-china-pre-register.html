How to pre-register for Valorant Mobile (China beta)

By Zibno · 10 May 2025

The safe way to pre-register for the Valorant Mobile China beta from outside China with a QQ or WeChat account, and why leaked APKs will not work.

Watch the video on YouTube

This guide was written for the Valorant Mobile China beta. Steps and availability can change, so check the date on the video before you start.

First: do not buy APKs

Valorant Mobile officially said that anyone using or sharing leaked APK files or game packages will not be able to play. If someone sells you a "leaked" version, you are paying for nothing. Do not fall for it.

What you need

  • A QQ or WeChat account. Registration for the China beta needs one of them.
  • A booster app with a built-in VPN to reach the Chinese services. I use LagoFast; download it from its official website, not from the Play Store.

Steps

  1. Install LagoFast from the official website. My code Zibno gives VIP access.
  2. Inside LagoFast, search for WeChat or QQ and boost it, then install WeChat or QQ.
  3. Sign up with your phone number or email.
  4. Back in LagoFast, search Valorant Mobile. Open the Valorant CN entry and its pre-register link.
  5. Log in with your QQ or WeChat account. You are registered.

Tip If WeChat or QQ asks you to scan a QR code to create the account, the app is either blocked in your country or wants an existing user to verify you. If you cannot get past that step, this method will not work for you, and I would rather tell you that than waste your time.

After registering

If you are picked, you get a message in QQ or WeChat saying you can download. If the game opens publicly in China, iOS players get it from the Chinese App Store, and Android players from TapTap (Chinese version) or other Chinese Android stores. LagoFast also updates its game entry once the beta is live.
