Why this site exists

My name is Zibno, and I make videos about mobile shooters. I cover Valorant Mobile, Delta Force, Apex Legends Mobile, High Energy Heroes and whatever drops next, with a focus on games that release in China or in closed beta first, when the menus are still in Chinese and nobody has explained them yet.

Every guide here is based on one of my videos or on official sources, which I link. I play on my own phone, I don't publish settings I haven't looked at, and I update guides when games change.
