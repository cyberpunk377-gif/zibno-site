About Zibno

I'm Zibno, an FPS gaming creator focused on mobile shooters: Valorant Mobile, Delta Force, Apex Legends Mobile, High Energy Heroes and whatever drops next.

I'm known for getting into games early. When a shooter launches first in China or in a closed beta, I show you how to download it, then translate every setting from Chinese to English and explain what it does, so you can play it properly from day one.

Around that I make settings guides, gyro and handcam tips, and quick Shorts. Game, learn, improve, together. More about me and how I make guides.
